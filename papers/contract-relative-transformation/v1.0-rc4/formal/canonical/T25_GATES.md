# Continuation: T25–T26

The finite reduction to T19 is intentionally the first gate, not an assertion
of arbitrary reflective closure.

1. Verify `MetaInvestigation.lean` (all 30 audit requests), including the
   fallback-potential proof. If it fails, repair proof bodies without weakening
   the statements. Keep the immutable parent package.
2. Close the active-core antichain invariant and the sufficient-budget theorem
   for unlimited compulsory learning separately. T25's bounded prefix +
   funded fallback bypasses, but does not prove, the latter.
3. Turn the cost table into an executable, charged evidence-acquisition path:
   immutable table cells from certified traces, exact task identity, replay,
   provenance; distinguish declared fee from measured runtime. Use interval
   bounds if exact costs are unavailable; never optimize unknown cells as zero.
4. Add a finite observation contract for meta-features and explicit reserve
   feasibility. A cost estimate should not expose hidden-world identity or
   the selected answer. Observation and estimation overhead must be charged.
5. Transfer a pruning *schema* via its universal soundness proof. To transfer
   a learned core, use T24's certified constraint/world reflection. To transfer
   an economical *policy*, construct a T20 morphism between the actual meta
   tasks, with verified port/price/loss relations; soundness alone is not enough.
6. Investigate an online policy portfolio when future meta-worlds are unknown.
   Include no-learning/no-meta controls, worst-case regret and amortized setup,
   selection, acquisition and retention charges. No general online advantage
   follows from the offline finite optimizer.
7. T26: proposals for replacing or synthesizing a controller must carry an
   independent admission certificate against the unchanged root contract.
   Preserve the reason/trace of revisions. A proposal does not certify itself.
   Prove that a meta-level switch preserves the remaining obligations and
   charge its cost; separately prove any claimed termination or strict gain.
