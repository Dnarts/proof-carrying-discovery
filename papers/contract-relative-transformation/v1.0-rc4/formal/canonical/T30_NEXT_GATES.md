# Fixed closure plan — T30 -> T31 -> T32

## Gate T30 — verify the finite episode

Compile MetaPolicy.lean and audit all 503 reports. Keep all 17 parent modules
unchanged. Preserve actual observation and paid preparation, the old reserve,
the T29b gate, the exact comparison of pre/post-payment frames, the selected
T19 legal tree, and the closed strict saving. A Python pass is not a kernel
pass. If any new proof fails, repair only this module or split the proof
obligations without changing the release scope or the statements silently.
The end-to-end witness is for the declared abstract tariffs, not physical
runtime. T29a/b/c stay verified independently of this gate.

## Gate T31 — compositional closure, not another meta-level

Keep the provider, observation semantics, tariff basis, reference task,
root checker and meta-checker fixed. Use actual T30 transitions plus the
T29c trace interface. Quantify over finite connected histories of meta-policy
upgrades; the installed policy may change arbitrarily often within the
admitted finite language. The frame remains indexed by the same old
continuation during the episode. A successful policy upgrade carries a strict
all-in improvement relative to its specific preceding policy; rejection is
paid, and no global gain is assumed for a mixed history. Ordinary episode
execution preserves the original root obligations by the existing laws.
Retain every paid attempt and query response plus prepared offer (including
when validation was deferred); append the terminal episode events to the
history of the whole run. Do not claim a decoder for external serialized
proofs. Prove preservation of reference membership, prior certificates,
resource conservation, liquid fallback, and append-only histories via
composition. Bound paid upgrades by their positive fees and balance; an
explicit finite fuel or halt condition also bounds free deferred requests.
If needed use an ordinary list of connected steps, never a dependent
computed endpoint constructor. One quantified invariant is the goal, not
unbounded new controller types. Policy synthesis is finite and supplied
certificates remain proof obligations, not a free oracle.

## Gate T32 — release

One executable entry point for the admitted finite reference episode or
bounded sequence of episodes; explicit terminal outcome (completed or honest
unresolved continuation), preserved root/fallback, exact resource ledger,
retained evidence and policy history; independent verifier and complete
manifest; end-to-end reproducible positive and negative controls and all
Lean proofs audited. Report the declared modeled tariffs and offline
certificate/model preparation limitations separately from real elapsed time.
Use the same finite domain, immutable root contract, and unchanged T29
semantics. Freeze the architecture after this gate. No new mandatory
research stage. Production persistence/crash recovery, stochastic ports,
new hypothesis synthesis, unrestricted policy rewrite, measured full-lifetime
amortization and broader transfer remain subsequent research, not implicit
claims of this release. If a compulsory gate cannot be established, record
it as an explicit blocking limitation rather than extending the roadmap.
